Not the commit in my earlier note. Take github.com/0xashot/wealthsmith @

While checking your deployment I found a real bug, and it was mine, not yours.
getSignatureStatuses reports a transaction as landed several seconds before
getTransaction can return it. I caught a Jupiter swap at the moment of landing
and your endpoint returned fee=null for five seconds straight, then returned
5703 lamports matching the chain exactly — correct behaviour, nothing to fix on
your side.

The problem is that our client asks at exactly that instant: the first poll
that sees a terminal state, and then it stops. So the success screen would
often have said "we couldn't load the exact USDC amount received" about a swap
whose amount was available one second later.

Fixed client-side in c5cb3ed: the outcome is reported immediately, and the
figures get four more attempts 1.5s apart. Your endpoint and its contract are
unchanged.
