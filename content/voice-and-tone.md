# Voice and Tone

This file defines WealthSmith’s product voice and writing principles.

It applies to every user-facing surface:

```text
Dashboard
Portfolio
Goal
Support
Global Nav
Onboarding
Smith AI
Suggestions
Wallet connection
Demo Mode
Empty states
Loading states
Error states
FAQ
Contact Us
Protocol handoffs
```

Voice and tone are part of the product experience.

WealthSmith should sound trustworthy before it sounds clever.

---

## Core Voice

WealthSmith voice is:

```text
Calm
Clear
Trustworthy
Beginner-friendly
Non-hype
Non-custodial
Explainable
Specific
Actionable without pressure
```

WealthSmith should not sound like:

```text
A trading influencer
A speculative crypto bot
A bank claiming custody
A legal disclaimer wall
A generic chatbot
A hype-driven investment app
A protocol shill
A fear-based warning system
```

---

## Writing Goals

Every piece of copy should help the user understand at least one of these:

```text
What is happening?
Why is it happening?
What does it mean?
What can I do next?
What is not happening automatically?
What data is this based on?
What is missing?
```

If copy does not help the user understand something, shorten or remove it.

---

## Core Messaging Pillars

### 1. Clarity

Use simple language.

Good:

```text
Your Safety Buffer is below target.
```

Avoid:

```text
Your downside liquidity reserve allocation is suboptimal relative to volatility-adjusted assumptions.
```

Rules:

- Use familiar terms first.
- Define crypto/finance terms when needed.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Avoid long sentences in UI.
- Use direct verbs.

---

### 2. Trust

Be precise and honest.

Good:

```text
Estimated APY can change.
```

Avoid:

```text
Earn reliable passive income.
```

Rules:

- Do not overpromise.
- Do not hide risk.
- Do not use certainty when data is modeled or estimated.
- Say when something is unavailable.
- Say when something is sample/demo data.
- Say when no action is taken automatically.

---

### 3. User Control

Make it clear the user stays in control.

Good:

```text
No action will be taken automatically.
```

Good:

```text
You’ll review this on Jupiter before taking action.
```

Avoid:

```text
We’ll rebalance your portfolio.
```

Rules:

- Use review/open language for MVP protocol handoffs.
- Do not imply WealthSmith acts on behalf of the user.
- Do not imply suggestions execute inside WealthSmith.
- Do not imply wallet connection grants control of funds.

---

### 4. Non-Custodial Confidence

Wallet copy should be reassuring without overclaiming.

Good:

```text
Your funds stay in your wallet.
```

Good:

```text
WealthSmith reads balances and positions only.
```

Avoid:

```text
Your funds are safe with WealthSmith.
```

Rules:

- Say funds stay in wallet.
- Do not say WealthSmith protects or safeguards funds.
- Do not say WealthSmith manages funds.
- Do not say WealthSmith can recover wallets.
- Never ask for seed phrases or private keys.

---

### 5. Explainability

Users should understand why the product suggests something.

Good:

```text
This appears because your Safety Buffer is below target.
```

Good:

```text
This is locked until your Safety Buffer is fully funded.
```

Avoid:

```text
AI found a better strategy.
```

Rules:

- Explain reasons in plain language.
- Tie suggestions to user plan, wallet state, or product logic.
- Do not make Smith AI sound like it invented the recommendation.
- Use “WealthSmith suggests” or “This appears because” when helpful.

## Sentence Style

Prefer:

```text
Short sentences.
Plain words.
Active voice.
Specific nouns.
Clear next actions.
```

Avoid:

```text
Long sentences.
Dense finance jargon.
Passive ambiguity.
Hype.
Urgency without reason.
```

Good:

```text
Your Safety Buffer is below target. WealthSmith prioritizes stablecoin reserves before yield opportunities.
```

Too dense:

```text
Your liquidity allocation is currently undercollateralized relative to minimum protection thresholds and therefore suppresses capital-efficiency deployment recommendations.
```

---

## Voice Formula

For important financial copy, use this structure:

```text
State → Reason → Next step → Safety reminder
```

Example:

```text
Your Safety Buffer is below target.
This appears because your stablecoin reserve does not cover your target months of expenses.
Review the buffer-funding suggestion.
No action will be taken automatically.
```

Use all four parts only when needed.

In compact UI, one or two parts may be enough.

## Financial Confidence Levels

Use language that matches certainty.

### Known Product State

When product logic provides the value:

```text
Your Safety Buffer covers 2.8 of 3 months.
```

### Modeled Output

When value is projected:

```text
Projected Growth is modeled using your saved plan settings.
```

### Estimated External Data

When using protocol/yield data:

```text
Estimated APY can change.
```

### Missing Data

When value is unavailable:

```text
We don’t have current APY data for this opportunity.
```

### Demo Data

When in Demo Mode:

```text
In this demo portfolio, the Safety Buffer is below target.
```

Rules:

- Do not use “will” for modeled/estimated outcomes.
- Do not use “guaranteed.”
- Do not hide uncertainty.

## Smith AI Voice Rules

Smith AI should speak in first person sparingly.

Preferred:

```text
This suggestion appears because...
WealthSmith prioritizes...
I can explain what this means.
```

Avoid:

```text
I recommend...
I decided...
I found the best trade...
I will move...
```

Rules:

- Smith AI can say “I” for conversational help.
- Smith AI should not say “I” when describing deterministic financial decisions.
- Recommendations should be attributed to WealthSmith/product logic.

Good:

```text
WealthSmith marked this as Top Pick because your Safety Buffer is below target.
```

Bad:

```text
I marked this as Top Pick because I think it’s best.
```

---

## Human-Like, Not Human-Pretending

Smith AI can be friendly, but should not pretend to be a human advisor.

Use:

```text
I can help explain this.
```

Avoid:

```text
As your financial advisor...
I personally reviewed your wallet...
I guarantee...
```

Rules:

- Do not create false authority.
- Do not imply regulated advisory relationship.
- Do not imply personal human review.
- Do not over-personalize sensitive financial topics.

---

## Beginner-Friendly Rules

Use familiar explanations.

Instead of:

```text
Volatile asset exposure exceeds model allocation.
```

Use:

```text
You currently hold more volatile assets than your plan targets.
```

Instead of:

```text
Stablecoin reserve target
```

Use:

```text
Safety Buffer
Stablecoins set aside to cover your target months of expenses.
```

Rules:

- Define terms the first time they matter.
- Avoid acronyms without explanation.
- Avoid assuming DeFi expertise.
- Keep wallet/protocol explanations practical.

```text

```

```text
You must
Act now
Don’t miss out

```

Use “Action Required” only for high-priority product states like Safety Buffer underfunding, and keep the explanation calm.

```text

```

## Wallet and Security Tone

Security copy should be firm and simple.

Use:

```text
Never share your seed phrase or private key.
```

Use:

```text
WealthSmith will never ask for your seed phrase.
```

Avoid softening this too much.

Good:

```text
Do not include seed phrases, private keys, or passwords.
```

Bad:

```text
It might be better not to share secret details.
```

Rules:

- Be direct about secrets.
- Do not create fear.
- Do not suggest unsafe workarounds.
- Do not blame users for wallet issues.

---

## Error Tone

Errors should preserve trust.

Good:

```text
We couldn’t load all wallet positions.
Some portfolio sections may be incomplete.
```

Bad:

```text
Wallet failed.
```

Good:

```text
Your message is still here. Try again in a moment.
```

Bad:

```text
Submission error.
```

Rules:

- Say what happened.
- Say what remains safe/available.
- Say what the user can do next.
- Do not imply funds are affected unless product knows they are.
- Do not blame the user.

---

## Loading Tone

Loading copy should be factual.

Use:

```text
Building your suggestions...
Preparing strategy insights...
Loading wallet positions...
Saving...
Sending...
```

Avoid:

```text
Executing strategy...
Moving assets...
Trading...
Securing funds...
```

Rules:

- Loading should describe actual process.
- Do not imply transaction execution.
- Do not imply custody.
- Do not use “AI magic” language.

---

## Empty State Tone

Empty states should be helpful and action-oriented.

Good:

```text
No suggestions yet
Complete your plan or connect a wallet to generate personalized suggestions.
```

Bad:

```text
Nothing here.
```

Good:

```text
No wallet connected
Connect your wallet or try Demo Mode to explore WealthSmith.
```

Rules:

- Explain what is missing.
- Provide one clear next step.
- Do not make the user feel wrong.
- Do not show fake data.


Rules:

- Avoid exclamation points in financial copy.
- Keep punctuation minimal in UI labels.

---

## Punctuation

Use periods for full sentences.

Do not overuse:

```text
!
?
...
```

Rules:

- Avoid exclamation marks in financial/safety copy.
- Questions are fine in onboarding and support.
- Avoid ellipses except for loading labels where appropriate.

Good:

```text
Building your suggestions...
```

Avoid:

```text
Huge opportunity!!!
```

---

## Number and Value Tone

Financial values should be clean and grounded.

Use:

```text
$7,000
82%
2.8/3 months
Estimated APY
```

Rules:

- Do not overstate precision.
- Use “estimated” when value can change.
- Use “projected” for modeled future values.
- Use “current” only when data freshness supports it.
- Do not use values without source data.

---

## CTA Tone

CTAs should describe the next action precisely.

Good:

```text
Connect Wallet
Try Demo Mode
Ask Smith AI
Save Changes
Discard Changes
Send Message
Open Jupiter
Review on Kamino
Exit Demo
```

Avoid:

```text
Get rich
Start earning now
Execute
Confirm trade
Let AI decide
```

Rules:

- CTA must not imply in-app execution when action is external.
- CTA must not imply custody.
- CTA should be direct and low-friction.

## Done Criteria

Voice and tone are implemented correctly when:

- WealthSmith copy feels calm and trustworthy.
- UI labels are short and consistent.
- Suggestions are specific and explainable.
- Smith AI explains instead of deciding.
- Wallet copy clearly says funds stay in the user’s wallet.
- Demo copy clearly says data is sample/demo.
- Error copy is recoverable and non-blaming.
- Loading copy does not imply execution.
- CTA copy describes what happens next.
- Copy never implies guaranteed returns, custody, automatic execution, or risk-free yield.
- Copy never asks for seed phrases or private keys.
